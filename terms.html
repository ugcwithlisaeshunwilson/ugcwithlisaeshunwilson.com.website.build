3. What We Do

UGC with Lisa creates content for brands, plain and simple. That includes short-form video in formats like product demos, testimonials, b-roll with voiceover, greenscreen, skit style, and more. It also includes UGC photography and written content like captions. Each project is scoped individually with its own deliverables, timeline, and pricing.
